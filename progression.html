PROGRESSION

BUILD POWER. KEEP THE FACTS CLEAN.

WARDEN confirms three broad progression ideas: discover creatures, collect cards, and collect plushies to become stronger. Exact values and systems are left to the live game.

3 confirmed pillarsNo drop-rate claims
PILLAR 01

Discover creatures

New creature discoveries expand the choices connected to future runs. The public description does not state a specific unlock tree or sequence.

PILLAR 02

Collect cards

Cards are explicitly named as collectibles that help you grow stronger. Their categories, values, and acquisition details are not published here without verification.

PILLAR 03

Collect plushies

Plushies are also part of confirmed progression. Use the current in-game descriptions to compare what you actually own.

GOOD PRACTICE

Judge one change at a time

When testing a new choice, compare how the run feels before and after. Avoid treating a single good run as proof of an undocumented bonus.
