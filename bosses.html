BOSS ESCAPES

THE GOAL IS ESCAPE

The official description confirms a boss escape, but does not name the boss or publish a route, health value, phase count, attack list, or exact timing.

Verified objectiveEscape the boss
Do not memorize an unsupported script

Observe the current encounter and follow what the game communicates. Any boss-specific claim needs a current official source before it belongs in this guide.

BEFORE

Regroup and share what you know

Before the pressure rises, make sure the group is working from the same task information and that no important clue has been missed.

DURING

Call danger, direction, decision

Use short information that helps immediately. Avoid burying a useful warning in a long explanation while the team is moving.

WHEN UNSURE

Read the encounter

Watch the environment, task feedback, and teammate movement. This advice stays valid without claiming a specific attack pattern.

AFTER

Separate evidence from guesses

After the run, compare what everyone directly observed. A repeatable observation is more valuable than a confident rumor.

ENTER WITH A PLAN

Go deeper. Stay together. Find the way out.

Open the official Roblox experience when your group is ready.
