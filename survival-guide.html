PLAYER STRATEGY

SURVIVE THE UNKNOWN

These habits are general team advice built around the confirmed WARDEN loop. They are not hidden stats, guaranteed tactics, or official instructions.

Team habitsNo invented mechanics
The simple rule

See together, decide together, move together. Good communication survives game updates better than a brittle route or unverified trick.

FIELD NOTE 01

Observe before committing

An unfamiliar place rewards a brief scan. Notice the current task, likely paths, visible threats, and where your teammates are before the group advances.

FIELD NOTE 02

Use short callouts

Share the useful part first: danger, task, clue, direction, or regroup. Clear information helps a team respond without pretending there is one official communication system.

FIELD NOTE 03

Keep the group recoverable

Avoid stretching so far apart that discoveries cannot be shared. Staying together does not mean standing in the same spot; it means keeping the team able to respond.

FIELD NOTE 04

Protect task focus

Exploration is part of the game, but so are tasks. When the group finds an active objective, agree on who is watching, searching, or helping before wandering farther.

FIELD NOTE 05

Treat clues as shared evidence

A puzzle detail may make more sense alongside something another player noticed. State what you saw before jumping to a conclusion.

FIELD NOTE 06

React to the boss as a team

The verified goal is escape. Exact boss behavior is not published here, so watch the current run, keep calls simple, and adjust together.
