HOW A RUN UNFOLDS

WARDEN is built around choosing a creature, exploring with a group, completing tasks under pressure, uncovering secrets, and escaping a boss. This guide keeps the route clear without inventing mechanics.

6 confirmed stagesSpoiler-light overview
What to expect

Locations, task details, controls, and conditions should be learned from the current game interface. They are not stated in the official public description we verified.

PHASE 01

Choose a creature

The official description begins with creature selection. Use the information shown inside the game rather than an unofficial tier claim.

PHASE 02

Form your group

WARDEN is presented as a team experience. Join friends and agree to share discoveries, task information, and danger calls.

PHASE 03

Enter the unknown

Each run takes place in an unfamiliar location and asks the group to travel deeper. Pause long enough to notice what the game communicates.

PHASE 04

Avoid danger and do the work

Monsters and tasks are both part of the confirmed loop. Keep the group focused on the objective instead of scattering without a plan.

PHASE 05

Solve and investigate

Puzzles and secrets reward attention. Compare what teammates have seen before deciding what a clue means.

PHASE 06

Escape the boss

A boss escape closes the danger loop. The official description does not document a specific boss, route, timer, or input sequence.
